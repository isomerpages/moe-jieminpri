<h2>What is SLS?</h2>
<p>The Singapore Student Learning Space, or SLS, is MOE's core platform for
teaching and learning and is one of MOE’s key initiatives to transform
the learning experiences of Singaporean students through the purposeful
use of technology to enable students to engage in different learning modes.</p>
<p>By logging to SLS, Jieminites have access to curriculum-aligned resources.
They can learn anytime, anywhere, and at their own pace. The SLS is also
integral for our teachers to design lessons and curate digital resources.</p>
<p>We look forward to your support as we help our students strengthen their
learning through the SLS.</p>
<p>Below are some of the common issues faced by students when logging in
to SLS:</p>
